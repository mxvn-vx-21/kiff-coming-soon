// faq

Questions, answered.

Is Kiff free?

Yes. Building, publishing, and hosting on a kiffstudio.in subdomain is free forever. Premium templates and AI usage beyond the free monthly quota are paid.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Drag blocks in the editor or describe what you want to the AI. A visual editor handles the rest. You can also export the full HTML/CSS/TS source anytime.

Which AI models power the Studio?

Claude Code (Anthropic), Codex / GPT (OpenAI), GLM 5.2 (Z.ai), and Grok (xAI) — with more providers coming. Pick the right model for each task, or let Kiff choose.

Can I sell my templates?

Yes. Anyone can list templates on the marketplace. Creators keep 85% of every sale, paid out monthly via Stripe Connect.
